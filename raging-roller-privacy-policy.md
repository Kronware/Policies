# Privacy Policy — Raging Roller

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Raging Roller game ("the App"), provided by
Kronware ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information.

---

## Information We Collect

### Game progress and settings stored on your device
The App saves your settings (such as tilt calibration, audio volumes, and
selected theme and marble skin), your course progress, unlocked cosmetics,
and daily-challenge records. This data is stored **locally on your device only**.
The App never uploads it to our servers, and we don't operate any servers.

### Trusted date for daily challenges
To choose the daily challenge, the App asks a public time service
([timeapi.io](https://timeapi.io) or [worldtimeapi.org](https://worldtimeapi.org))
for the current UTC date. These requests contain no personal or game data. As
with any web request, the time service receives your IP address.

### Crash reports
The App uses **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) to collect crash reports so we
can find and fix problems. If the App crashes, Crashlytics may send the
following data:
- The stack trace.
- Device model and operating-system version.
- App version, and memory and disk state.
- The time of the crash.
- A Firebase installation identifier, which is unique to this installation of
  the App.

Crash reports don't include your game progress or settings. Google processes
this data as described in the
[Firebase Privacy and Security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
and keeps Crashlytics crash data for 90 days.

### Google Play Games leaderboards (optional)
If you sign in to **Google Play Games**, the App sends your run scores to the
Raging Roller High Score leaderboard. The App shows your Play Games display name
on the High Scores screen but doesn't store it or send it anywhere. Google
processes your Play Games profile and scores under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your Play Games
profile visibility settings control who can see your scores. You can play
without signing in.

### Advertising
The App shows advertisements through **Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads**,
which mediates advertising networks. Unity/ironSource and its advertising
partners may collect the following data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud
prevention, and service reliability:
- Device and advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
- Your IP address, and an approximate location inferred from it.
- Device and network information.
- Your interactions with ads.
- Technical logs.

This data may be shared with advertising, bidding, and service partners. See
[Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)
for retention, privacy choices, and access/deletion rights. The App does not
request precise-location or Bluetooth permissions. Test ads do not imply that the
advertising SDK collects no data.

To limit personalised advertising, use your device's advertising-ID controls
(**Settings > Privacy > Ads** on most Android devices) and the opt-out choices
described in Unity's policy.

---

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- The App has no account system of its own and doesn't ask for your name, email
  address, or phone number. Optional Google Play Games sign-in is handled by
  Google.
- The App doesn't request precise location. Advertising providers may infer an
  approximate location, as described above.
- The App doesn't use the camera, microphone, contacts, or your files. Motion
  sensors are used only for tilt controls, on the device.
- We don't sell your game data. The advertising provider's sharing and sale
  practices, and your opt-out rights, are described in its policy.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the App or
clear its storage in Android settings. Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days.

Kronware has no accounts or servers, so we don't hold your data and can't delete
data held by Google or Unity:
- **Leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile** are managed in your Google
  account (**Play Games app > Settings**).
- **Crashlytics data** is held by Google and processed under
  [Firebase's privacy terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Advertising data** retention and deletion are governed by
  [Unity's policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).
